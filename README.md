### PhD Candidate in Physics | Theoretical High-Energy Nuclear Physics | Quantum Computing

I am a PhD candidate at **Kent State University** (expected 2026). My work focuses on the phenomenology of **Heavy-Ion Collisions**, specifically the transport and suppression of heavy quarkonia (bottomonium/charmonium) in the **Quark-Gluon Plasma (QGP)** using **Open Quantum Systems (OQS)**. 

Beyond classical simulations, I am exploring **Quantum Advantage** by developing state-preparation algorithms for **SU(2) Lattice Gauge Theories** on quantum hardware.

---

### 🔬 Core Research & Expertise

* **Quarkonium Transport:** Expertise in modeling $\Upsilon$ suppression in $p\text{--}Pb$ and $AA$ collisions at RHIC and LHC energies.
* **Open Quantum Systems:** Implementing Lindblad equation solvers for real-time quantum dynamics in hot nuclear matter.
* **Lattice Gauge Theory:** Developing VQE and state-preparation workflows for non-Abelian gauge theories using Qiskit.
* **High-Performance Computing:** Scaling simulations on **NERSC (Perlmutter)** and **Ohio Supercomputer Center (OSC)** clusters.
